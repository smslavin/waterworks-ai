# O&M Manual Excerpt — Clarifier and UV Disinfection

## Clarifier — Level and Turbidity

Normal clarifier level is 20–90%. Level below 20% risks air entrainment
into the downstream process; level above 90% risks carryover into the
clearwell. Normal outlet turbidity is 0–4 NTU. A turbidity spike combined
with a stable level usually indicates a settling upset (coagulant dosing
drift or a hydraulic short-circuit through the basin) rather than a
mechanical fault — check upstream chemical dosing pump status before
escalating.

## UV Disinfection — Intensity and Lamp Hours

UV intensity must stay within 80–100% of rated output to guarantee dose.
Intensity degrading gradually over weeks while lamp hours climb toward the
9,000-hour rated life is expected lamp aging — plan replacement before
intensity drops below 80%, don't wait for a low-intensity alarm. A sudden
intensity drop (not gradual) at any lamp-hour count indicates lamp failure
or a fouled quartz sleeve, not end-of-life aging, and should be treated as
an immediate disinfection risk.

## Safety

Lockout-tagout is required before opening any UV reactor vessel — UV lamps
remain hazardous to unprotected skin and eyes even when reported as "off"
by the control system until physically de-energized and verified.
