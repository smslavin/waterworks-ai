# Pump Operating Limits — RawWater_01 / RawWater_02

## Flow

Normal sustained flow range is 200–450 L/min. Sustained operation above
450 L/min accelerates impeller wear and increases cavitation risk,
particularly when suction-side pressure is already marginal. Sustained
operation below 200 L/min for more than 15 minutes indicates a possible
suction starvation condition and should be investigated before the pump
trips on low flow protection.

## Pressure

Discharge pressure must remain within 3.5–8.5 bar. A pressure reading that
drops sharply while flow stays constant is the earliest indicator of
cavitation — check NPSH available before assuming a sensor fault.

## Power

Normal power draw is 10–70 kW. Power draw climbing while flow and pressure
stay flat usually points to bearing wear or impeller fouling, not a process
condition — schedule a mechanical inspection rather than adjusting setpoints.

## Cavitation response

If cavitation is suspected: reduce flow demand first (do not increase
discharge throttling, which worsens suction conditions), confirm suction
strainer is not blocked, and verify wet well level is within the
manufacturer's minimum submergence spec before resuming normal operation.
